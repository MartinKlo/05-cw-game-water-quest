This is the concept for the game that we are creating.

"Well Spotter - Game Logic:
1. Game Start:
- Display Logo and have start game button
- When game is started: Load image of well on the left. On the right, there is a map and a slider for how many people the well served.
2. Player Actions:
- The player can hover over the map and it will expand. When the player clicks on a point in the map, they can place down their guess. The player can use the slider to guess how many people were served by a well. These will set the values for the guess, and the guess will be made by clicking the “Guess” button.
3. Game Logic:
- The game will watch for the user’s mouse position, as well as where they click on the map. It will also calculate how far the guesses are from the correct answer (coordinates and people served)
4. Score/Feedback:
- Score is calculated with each guess and then added to the total. The game will show the user how far their guess was from the original point, as well as provide a description of the well.
5. Win or Lose Conditions:
- The game ends when the player has gone through 5 images. The game will show a summary score at the end with a button to play again.
6. Reset/Replay:
- The player can either hit the play again button, or return home and play through there."
